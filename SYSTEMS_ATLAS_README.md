## Purpose

Systems Atlas is an ambitious, interactive **personal engineering portfolio**, centred on the work and professional experience of David Willsher. It must **not** become a For The Table marketing website. FTT is one case study among several and already has its own product website.

**Visual direction accepted:** The Systems Atlas concept, with editorial/architectural restraint, unusually thoughtful interaction, accessibility and performance. The existing diagrams and HTML experiments are **probes, not final designs**. Feedback: architecture diagrams felt crowded/hard on the eyes; they did not yet achieve the ambition of a remarkable spatial experience.

## Current content strategy

- **Portfolio/homepage:** Who David is, what problems he solves, engineering approach and selected work. The site's signature interactions should be portfolio-wide, not FTT-specific.
- **First feature:** For The Table. Complete the written engineering case study and evidence selection **before** further exhibit design.
- **Further features:** AXIOM (bounded AI decision engine), Birria Radar (evidence-led lead research; still under active development), PAF (agentic workflow and development evaluation).
- **Professional experience:** Work-derived descriptions must avoid employer IP, confidential customer information and unapproved unreleased product details.
- **Website as an engineering showcase:** Document design decisions, implementation approach, testing, accessibility and performance without inventing evidence.

## What is *not* authorised

No production portfolio build, deployment, public launch announcement, changes to the private For The Table codebase, paid API usage, or publishable claims about commercial adoption.

For an actual autonomous development programme, use the **current** planning contract from the private agent-framework repository and prepare project-owned specification, acceptance and build-plan documents for separate review. This archive is not itself a PAF execution grant.
